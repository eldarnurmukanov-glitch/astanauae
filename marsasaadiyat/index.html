The district

Saadiyat's final large-scale release

Marsa Al Saadiyat is Aldar's closing masterplan on Saadiyat Island: a coastal destination built around a 177,000 m² marina, a 1 km dining promenade and 5.6 km of beachfront — minutes from Louvre Abu Dhabi, the Guggenheim, Zayed National Museum and NYU Abu Dhabi.

Largest marina in Abu Dhabi

177,000 m² with over 350 berths, from sailing boats to superyachts, lined with waterfront restaurants and a yacht club.

Dar Al Funoon

A 6,000+ seat Frank Gehry-designed venue for musicals, live productions and international performances.

Promenade & beach

A 1 km waterfront promenade with restaurants and leisure, a beach club and 5.6 km of beachfront.

Education & care

3 schools, 6 nurseries and 2 clinics within the district; Cranleigh and NYU Abu Dhabi close by on Saadiyat.

Connected living

46 km of cycling tracks, 140 km of walking trails and a future Etihad Rail station linking Abu Dhabi and beyond.

Green by design

A 73,800 m² central park and 129 ha of open space, with neighbourhood parks and landscaped corridors.
