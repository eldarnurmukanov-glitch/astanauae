Process

How it works

Four steps. You always know where the deal stands.

  1. Send us the contact

    Message us on WhatsApp or use the form below. A short description is enough to start.

  2. We register it to you

    We check our database and confirm in writing that the client or the property is registered to you.

  3. We run the deal

    Consultation, shortlist, viewings, negotiation, SPA. We update you at each key stage.

  4. You get paid

    Your share is paid by bank transfer once we receive our commission from the developer or the seller.

Terms

Programme rules

Simple rules, agreed in writing with every partner.

  1. Up to 50% of our commission

    The exact share depends on the deal type and your role. We agree it in writing before we start.

  2. Registered to you from the first introduction

    We confirm in writing that the client or the property is registered to you.

  3. Honest check against our database

    If the client or the property is already working with us, we tell you straight away.

  4. Paid when we are paid

    You receive your share once our commission arrives. If the deal does not close, nobody owes anything.

    We contact your client only with your agreement. All details stay between us.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need a real estate licence?

No. You do not need a licence to refer a client or a property. We sign a short referral agreement with every partner. Licensed brokers and agencies work with us on a co-broking agreement.

How is my share decided?

It is up to 50% of our commission and depends on the type of deal and how involved you are. A simple introduction earns less than active help through the deal. We agree the percentage in writing before we start.

When do I get paid?

After we receive our commission from the developer or the seller. On off-plan deals the developer usually pays the agency after the buyer's down payment and the signed SPA. We tell you the expected timing at the start.

What if the client buys a different property?

The referral is tied to the client, not to one unit. A purchase through us within the term of the agreement counts.

Which properties qualify?

Off-plan and ready property in the UAE and Oman: apartments, villas, townhouses, plots and commercial units.

Can I take part from Kazakhstan or another country?

Yes, from anywhere. We work remotely, and in Kazakhstan we have a partner company, Platinum Real Estate LLP.
