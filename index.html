Process

Four steps, one adviser

Clear at every stage, whether you are in Abu Dhabi, Astana or anywhere else. Viewings in person or by video.

  1. Brief

    A 30-minute call on your goals, budget, timeline and residency plans.

  2. Shortlist

    Three to five options with the numbers: price per sq ft, payment schedule, expected yield and resale outlook.

  3. Secure

    Viewings, reservation, SPA and payments — handled step by step with the developer.

  4. Own

    Handover, leasing and ongoing support for as long as you hold the asset.
